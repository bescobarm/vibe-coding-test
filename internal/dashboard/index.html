Risk score

A purchase that passes every rule gets a score from 0 to 100. Each suspicious pattern adds points, and the total decides what happens:

0–39 Approved Nothing unusual enough to act on.
40–69 Flagged Approved, and a person reviews it afterwards.
70–100 Declined Too risky to approve.
